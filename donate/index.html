Donations and Charity

We are committed to giving back to our community through various charity initiatives and donation drives. Your support helps us make a positive impact in the lives of those in need.

To contribute, please participate in our charity events. Every contribution, big or small, makes a difference.

We also encourage you to consider making a direct donation to our charity partners.

For more information about what charities we work with, you can see our full list of partners below.

We also use donations to run our community benefits, such as hosting game servers and providing resources for our team.
