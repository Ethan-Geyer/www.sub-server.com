About Us

We’re a small but chaotic crew of about 6/7 streamers who rotate in and out at through out the week, hosting game servers, running live events, and keeping the energy alive no matter who’s online. What started as friends hanging out has grown into a safe, welcoming community where people can relax, have fun, and make new friends without worrying about judgment or drama. Whether we’re streaming, building worlds, or just laughing our way through another late‑night session, our goal is simple: create a space where everyone feels comfortable being themselves and having a good time.

Read more about us individually below.

MusclesMcsex

MusclesMcSex is the squad’s perfectly timed punchline machine — the guy who drops a joke so sharp it derails the entire lobby, then turns around and helps you out the moment you actually need him. He’s 10% skill, 90% aesthetic, and 0% qualified to be here, and somehow that’s exactly why he fits. Yes, the name is real. Yes, the pixels are too. Muscles is our satirical gaslighter and “professional” button‑masher, delivering top‑tier entertainment wrapped in bottom‑tier gameplay. Whether he’s cracking jokes, clutching by accident, or talking his way into (and out of) chaos, Muscles is the comedic backbone of the crew.

Crimson_Obsession

Crimson is the quiet storm of the squad: mysterious, calm, and collected even when the rest of us are actively trying to set the lobby on fire. She’s our squad mom, the one who organizes everything, keeps us on track, and valiantly (but hopelessly) attempts to maintain order in a place where chaos lingers and bad decisions feel right. If it’s toxic, she’s interested; if it’s messy, she’s already sighing and cleaning it up. Crimson brings that soft‑spoken authority, the “stay… you won’t want to leave” energy, and a level of patience none of us deserve. When she’s around, the madness feels a little more manageable, even if we immediately ruin it.

TheHuntsMen

I’m TheHuntsMen — the guy behind the Infrastructure, the servers, and the chaos. When I’m not diving into games like Schedule 1, Helldivers, or Elden Ring, I’m usually building something for the community. I designed and run our website, moderate and manage all of our game servers, and keep everything running smoothly so everyone else can just jump in and have fun. Whether I’m live on stream or working behind the scenes, I’m all about creating a place where people can hang out, laugh, and enjoy good games together.

Baronplayzs

Baron is the quiet constant of the squad — the red‑haired gamer who doesn’t need to talk big because his gameplay already does the heavy lifting. He’s the reliable one, the guy who can walk straight through the chaos we create and still come out with a clean clutch like it’s nothing. “Yo, I’m BaronPlayz — your red‑haired gamer bringing hype, humor, and high‑skill chaos to every stream” isn’t just a tagline; it’s exactly how he plays. Expect cracked moments, steady support, and reactions that hit harder because they’re rare. When Baron’s in the lobby, everything feels just a little more under control.

Grafto5489

Grafto is our resident loudmouth legend — the man, the myth, the seven‑toed Fortnite warrior. What he lacks in toes, he more than makes up for in sheer confidence and unhinged energy. He’s got the aim of a cracked gremlin, the volume of a jet engine, and somehow the audacity to steal Mr. Steal‑Your‑Girl’s girl. Nobody knows how he pulls it off — maybe it’s the swagger, maybe it’s the chaos aura — but whenever Grafto joins the squad, the lobby gets louder, funnier, and way less predictable.

LegendofDillian

Dillian is our horror‑loving wildcard — he doesn’t show up often, but when he does, it’s instantly a whole event. He brings big energy, big reactions, and that unmistakable “strap in, something’s about to happen” vibe. “Hey everyone. I’m TheLegendofDillian. I’m new to this whole streaming thing — join me on my adventure through this interesting world of streaming” might sound humble, but the moment he goes live, it’s pure entertainment. Whether he’s screaming through a horror game, cracking unexpected jokes, or flexing his DUBBY partnership (use code LGENDOFDILLIAN), every appearance from Dillian feels like a special drop — rare, chaotic, and absolutely worth catching.

NerdBird666

NerdBird is our resident medic, but don’t let the healing hands fool you — she’ll drop you in‑game, bring you back, and do it again just to make a point. She’s got a dangerous streak wrapped in a warm smile, the kind of player who can keep your health bar maxed while simultaneously reminding you she’s the real threat in the lobby. When you need food, heals, or a miracle, she’s the one to call. And outside the chaos, she’s our real‑life druid — nature‑loving, grounded, and somehow still patient enough to deal with the rest of us. In short: she’ll save you… after she’s done killing you.
