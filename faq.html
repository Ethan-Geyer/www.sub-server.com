How to use the Prime Sub?

Link your Amazon Prime to Twitch, go to the streamer’s channel, hit Subscribe, and choose “Subscribe Free with Prime.” That’s it — one free sub every month.

Follow VS Subscribe?

Following is free. It just means you want to keep up with the channel. You’ll see when we go live, but that’s it — no extra perks. Subscribing is a paid (or Prime) support option. Subs get actual benefits because they’re directly supporting the streamer. That usually includes: Ad‑free viewing, Exclusive emotes, "foot-pics", Sub‑only channels or Discord access, Badges next to your name, Special events or perks. Following shows support. Subscribing unlocks rewards.

I subscribed now what?

After you subscribe, you’ll receive a DM from either the streamer or a mod with your Discord access link. Once you’re in the Discord, you’ll find game server info, join links, updates, and more all in one place.

What do I get from teir rewards?

All tiers get full Discord access, including game servers, updates, events, and community perks, while higher tiers unlock more benefits: Tier 1 gives you Discord access along with basic sub perks like emotes, a badge, and ad‑free viewing; Tier 2 adds bonus emotes and extra community perks; and Tier 3 includes everything plus collab access, priority invites, and top‑tier community benefits, giving you more the higher you go.

Charity Streams?

Yes, we do charity streams — mostly led by Crimson. Whenever we’re raising money, all donation info will be shown directly on stream so you always know who we’re supporting and how to contribute.

Can I donate?

Yes — if we’re accepting donations, the links will be in our Twitch bios or shown during the stream. Everything you need will always be easy to find while we’re live.

What intagrations do we use?

We use a mix of tools like OBS, Streamlabs, Nightbot, and more to keep everything running smoothly. Each streamer on the team has their own setup and style, so things vary from person to person. If you’re curious about the exact gear or software we use, just ask during stream — we’re always happy to talk about it live.
